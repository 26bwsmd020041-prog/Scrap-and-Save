01 Where it happens

Of the 1.05 billion tonnes wasted in 2022, most of it never made it past someone's own kitchen.

02 Why it's not just a food problem

8–10%

$1T+

03 The target

UN Sustainable Development Goal 12.3 commits countries to halving per-capita food waste at the retail and consumer level, and cutting food losses along production and supply chains, by 2030. A handful of countries — including the UK and Japan — have already shown double-digit reductions are possible. Most of the rest of the world hasn't started measuring yet, which is step one before anyone can cut anything.
